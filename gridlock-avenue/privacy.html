Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Summary: Gridlock Avenue has no account and no server of its own. Your progress stays on your phone. The game is free because it shows ads, and the ad partner (Unity LevelPlay) and the ad networks it works with process some device data to show and measure those ads. You choose on first launch whether ads may be personalised, and you can change that choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy.

Who is responsible

Gridlock Avenue is developed by Mehmet Tunca Bildik ("we"). For any privacy question, write to tunca.bildik@gmail.com.

Information stored on your phone

So that the game can work, the following is kept only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • Your progress: the furthest exit reached, stars per exit and your coins
  • Your settings: sound and glow on or off
  • Your privacy choice and the date you made it

We never receive this information. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Advertising

The game shows rewarded video ads (which you choose to watch, for example for a free hint) and interstitial ads between some exits. Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies, and by the ad networks LevelPlay works with (currently ironSource Ads; more networks may be added later). To deliver, cap, measure and protect ads against fraud, these partners may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), only where you allow it
  • your IP address and the approximate location that can be derived from it
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language, screen size and app version
  • how you interact with ads, for example whether an ad was shown, completed or tapped

These partners act under their own privacy policies. Unity's policy is here: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. We do not receive any of this data in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated ad reports.

Your choice: personalised or non-personalised ads

Before any ad service starts, the game asks whether ads may be personalised. Both answers are equally easy and the game is the same either way.

  • Personalised ads: our partners may use the data above to choose ads that suit you. On iOS you then also see Apple's tracking prompt, and the advertising identifier is used only if you allow it there.
  • Non-personalised ads: you still see ads, but they are not chosen based on your data. On iOS the tracking prompt is not shown. Data may still be processed to deliver ads, count them and prevent fraud.

You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Legal basis

Personalised advertising relies on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR; explicit consent under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, KVKK). You may withdraw consent at any time under Settings → Privacy; withdrawal does not affect processing before it. Delivering non-personalised ads and preventing ad fraud rely on the legitimate interest in offering the game free of charge.

International transfers

Our ad partners may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. They are responsible for using appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to complain to a data protection authority (for example the Turkish KVK Authority or your EU supervisory authority). Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best sent to Unity through its privacy policy above; we will gladly help if you write to us.

Children

Gridlock Avenue is not directed at children under 13, and we tell our ad partners that the app is not directed at children. If you believe a child has used the game and data about them was processed, contact us and we will help arrange deletion with our partners.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above refreshed. Significant changes will also be shown in the game.
